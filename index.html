BorderPlus, since November 2025

Care, with less paperwork.

BorderPlus recruits and trains nurses from abroad and places them in German care homes. I lead its AI products, with a team of 13 engineers, designers and data scientists.

For nurses at work

  1. A nurse talks through what happened. CarePilot transcribes it, asks what is missing, and writes a German care report with a quality score. Documentation fell from 2.5 hours to 30 minutes per shift in three care homes.

  2. 88% of people who came in through Ask never found the courses in Learn, and nurses who used both came back 48% of the time, against 14%. This home joins the two.

  3. About 1,400 nurses use it and 968 keep coming back. A small model tags every conversation by need and by unanswered question, and those tags set the roadmap.

For nurses moving to Germany

  1. Type who you are and Pathways lays out the route to Germany step by step: placement, recognition, visa and arrival. The agentic version drafts the paperwork at each stage.

  2. The public face of the migration platform, with a page for each destination and what it takes to get there.

  3. A landing page for settlement support, so a nurse's first weeks in Germany don't undo the year it took to get there.

Zenea, a BorderPlus venture, 2026

Childcare you can follow from work.

A subscription where a trained nurse lives with the family and cares for children up to five. I designed both apps: one for the caregiver who logs the day, one for the parents who follow it.

  1. A start-of-day check, the day's routines as cards with checklists, and a voice log that fills them in while her hands are busy.

  2. Meals, naps, play and learning arrive as they happen, so parents at work see the day instead of asking about it.

Across all of it

What building with AI has taught me.

The products above share a few habits. Here they are, with where each one shows up.

  • Voice in, structured records out

    Speech is the fastest input for busy hands. The work is turning it into records that pass an audit.

    CarePilot, the Zenea voice log

  • Small models on real conversations

    A small model tags every conversation by need and by unanswered question. 78% of nurses who got no answer never came back, so filling gaps became a workstream.

    Ask

  • Defining good before tuning

    An annotation tool and an evaluation framework built on more than 10,000 care reports, so fine-tuning had a target.

    CarePilot

  • Agents with tools

    Existing models exposed as MCP tools, so an agent can answer and act in one conversation, with a person approving the action.

    Ads Agent

  • Local models for private words

    Some writing should never leave the device. Selah runs on a local model, with a deterministic safety check in front.

    Selah

  • Agents that remember

    Every project keeps its state in plain markdown, so any model can pick up where the last one stopped. I've written up how.
