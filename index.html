BorderPlus, since November 2025

Care, with less paperwork.

BorderPlus recruits and trains nurses from abroad and places them in German care homes. I lead its AI products, with a team of 13 engineers, designers and data scientists.

Ask

The assistant inside the NursePlus app. A nurse asks in German or English; Ask answers in a line, then opens the exact moment of the lesson film that covers it.

Nurses using it
~1,400
Engaged users
968
Come back, using Ask and Learn
48% vs 14%

How it works

  • Every answer points into Learn, down to the second where the film covers it, because the data showed 88% of people who came in through Ask never found Learn.
  • Films carry German, English or both as subtitles, and end with a speaking exercise.
  • Outside the app, I annotate conversations with a small language model for my own analysis. It showed that 78% of nurses who got no answer never came back, so filling those gaps became its own workstream.

Learn

German for the job, as pathways of short episodes: a first call from the airport, a day on the ward, opening a bank account, finding a home. A phrase trainer keeps the words close.

Week-2 and week-3 retention
up ~5%
Return rate with Ask and Learn together
48% vs 14%

How it works

  • Roleplay episodes with recurring characters, unit checks, streaks and daily goals.
  • Speaking practice is scored with Azure's pronunciation assessment.
  • A redesigned home, navigation and onboarding join Learn to Ask, so a question leads into a lesson.

CarePilot

Documentation for German care. A nurse picks the report, talks it through, checks the transcript, and gets a structured report with a quality score and the questions still open. Nurses had been writing these in unpaid overtime, and the records still failed audits.

Documentation per shift
2.5 h to 30 min
Live care homes
3

How it works

  • A documentation model fine-tuned on German care records turns what the nurse said into the report's own structure.
  • An annotation tool and evaluation framework I built: more than 10,000 annotated reports define what a good, compliant record is, and every report is scored against it.
  • Gaps are asked about before saving, so records meet German long-term care rules (SGB XI) before anyone signs them.
